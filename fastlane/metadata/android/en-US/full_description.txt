Aria is an open-source Android voice assistant that respects you.

Unlike the built-in assistants, Aria:

- Works fully offline for speech recognition (Vosk) and device commands —
  no account, no cloud required
- Can be set as your system default assistant
- Never tracks you: no analytics, no telemetry, no ads
- Lets you bring your own AI provider key (Groq, OpenRouter, Together,
  Ollama, LM Studio, ...) for conversational answers — keys are encrypted
  with the Android Keystore and excluded from cloud backup
- Understands spoken numbers and natural phrasing for device commands
  ("set an alarm for seven thirty a m", "turn on the flashlight")

Aria is built for low-power devices and does everything on your phone
whenever possible. Your voice never leaves the device unless you choose
a cloud AI provider yourself.

Open source under an OSI-approved license. Source code, issue tracker and
signed release APKs are on GitHub.
